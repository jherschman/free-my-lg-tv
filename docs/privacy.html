Privacy Policy

Free My TV for LG webOS (this fork) · Last updated November 1, 2026

Free My TV is an open-source home screen that runs on your own LG TV. This policy covers the Google sign-in it uses to show your Google Nest doorbell, cameras and thermostat on the TV.

What it accesses

With your permission, through Google's Smart Device Management API (Device Access) and Cloud Pub/Sub, the app reads the list of Nest devices you choose to share, doorbell and camera events (such as a ring), live camera streams while you are viewing them, and your thermostat's temperature and set point. It changes the thermostat's set point only when you do so on the TV.

Where your data goes

Nowhere but your TV and Google. The app has no server of its own. Your sign-in is stored only on your TV, inside the app's own storage, and the TV talks directly to Google. Camera video is streamed to the TV and is not recorded or saved. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign the app out at any time in your Google account under Third-party apps & services, or at Nest partner connections. Uninstalling the app from the TV deletes everything it stored.
